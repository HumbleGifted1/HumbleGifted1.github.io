# Connect or reconnect QuickBooks

StudentTracker connects to QuickBooks Online from the app on your computer:

1. Open **StudentTracker** on your Windows computer (the icon in the system tray, next to the clock).
2. Right-click the icon and choose **Connect QuickBooks**.
3. Sign in to QuickBooks in the browser window that opens, choose the company and click **Connect**.

Use the same steps to **reconnect** if StudentTracker says QuickBooks needs to be connected again
(for example after about 100 days without use).

StudentTracker only **reads** invoice information (customer email, created time, email status and sent
time). It never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks. See the [privacy policy](privacy).
