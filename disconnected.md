# StudentTracker has been disconnected from QuickBooks

StudentTracker can no longer read invoice information from your QuickBooks company. Nothing in
QuickBooks was changed.

- **To reconnect:** open StudentTracker on your Windows computer, right-click its tray icon and choose
  **Connect QuickBooks**. See [Connect or reconnect QuickBooks](connect).
- **To remove the data StudentTracker stored on a computer:** quit StudentTracker and delete the folder
  `%APPDATA%\StudentTracker` on that computer.
